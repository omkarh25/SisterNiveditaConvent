

### **Growth Story Timeline**

### **Admission Section**
#### **Admission Process**
- Admissions are based on **eligibility criteria** and an **interview process**.
- The school provides **co-education**, and the **curriculum follows the Karnataka State Syllabus**.

#### **Eligibility Criteria**
- **Nursery (LKG, UKG, and Infants)**: Minimum age criteria as per state education rules.
- **Primary to High School**: Admission requires **previous school records**, and for **transfer students**, a **Leaving Certificate** is mandatory.

#### **Application Process**
- Parents need to provide:
  - **Name, surname, father's name, date of birth, religion, caste, sub-caste**.
  - A **birth certificate issued by an authority** (for new admissions).
  - **Leaving certificate (LC) for transfer students** (duly signed by the department concerned).

#### **Fees**
- Fees must be **paid in advance** between the **1st and 12th of each month**.
- **Annual fees** for April and May are **collected in March**.
- **No student is allowed to sit for examinations unless fees are paid in full**.

#### **Scholarships**
- The school provides **concessions in fees** for **poor and needy students**.
- Applications for scholarships must be submitted **within the first fortnight of the academic year**.

#### **Enquiry Form**
- The school has an **enquiry form** for parents to reach out for **admissions and other details**.

### **Vision Section**
#### **Our Mission**
- The **school’s mission** is to **promote, support, and protect the dignity of each individual** as a **Child of God**.
- The **goal** is to ensure students' **academic, moral, and personal growth**.

#### **Our Vision**
- To **integrate physical, moral, intellectual, psychological, and social aspects** of a child’s growth.
- To **nurture future leaders** and **independent thinkers**.
- To make **education accessible** to students from **all backgrounds**.

#### **Our Values**
- **Education as Power**: The school follows the motto **"Education and Knowledge is Power"**.
- Focuses on **discipline, respect, and academic excellence**.
- Encourages **spiritual and moral development** alongside academics.
